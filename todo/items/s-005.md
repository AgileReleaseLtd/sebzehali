# s-005: AdSense

- Status: open
- Priority: P2
- Added: 2026-10-10

## What

After about four weeks of traffic: apply for AdSense. The privacy page then needs to cover cookies and consent.

## Notes

- 2026-10-13: the site now has a consent bar for Google Analytics (`static/cerez.js`) and the privacy
  page covers its cookies. Ads would need their own choice in that bar and their own section on the
  privacy page.
- 2026-10-24: the code is in place and switched off. Two settings in `sebzehali/config.py` turn it on:
  `ADSENSE_CLIENT` (adds the `google-adsense-account` meta tag and `ads.txt`, shows no ads) and
  `ADSENSE_SLOT` (adds one ad slot per price page, the ads choice in the consent bar and the ads
  section of the privacy page). AdSense loads only for visitors who accept ads.

## Steps

1. Around 2026-11-07: apply at adsense.google.com as Agile Release Ltd, with site `sebzehali.com`.
2. Put the publisher id (`ca-pub-…`) in `ADSENSE_CLIENT`, push, and check that
   `https://sebzehali.com/ads.txt` and `https://www.sebzehali.com/ads.txt` both answer. Then ask
   Google to review the site.
3. After approval: in AdSense, create one responsive display ad unit, put its id in `ADSENSE_SLOT`,
   set `PRIVACY_UPDATED` to that day, and push.
4. In AdSense → Privacy & messaging, turn on the European regulations message. Google requires its
   certified consent tool for visitors from the UK, the EU and Switzerland. Those visitors will see
   Google's message after accepting ads in our bar.
5. Don't turn on Auto ads: the slots are placed by hand, below the tables.

## Done when

Ads show to visitors who accept them, and AdSense reports no policy or ads.txt issues.
